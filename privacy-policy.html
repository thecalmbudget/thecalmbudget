1. Introduction

Welcome to The Calm Budget ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our products, or interact with us in any way.

Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, and other identifiers you voluntarily provide when you contact us, sign up for a newsletter, or purchase a product.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, and referring URLs — collected automatically via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Device Data: Browser type, operating system, IP address, and device identifiers.
  • Payment Data: Payment processing is handled by trusted third-party processors (e.g., Stripe, PayPal). We do not store full payment card details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver, operate, and improve our products and services.
  • Send transactional emails (order confirmations, download links).
  • Send marketing communications — only with your consent.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.
  • Prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and store information. You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some portions of our site may not function properly without cookies.

Third-party services we use (such as Google Analytics, Pinterest, or ad platforms) may place their own cookies on your device, subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors who assist us in operating our website, conducting business, or servicing you (e.g., email platforms, analytics, payment processors).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. When your data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent).
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the address below.

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete it.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every site you visit.

10. Security
