Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 3, 2026

1. Who We Are

The Calm Budget ("we," "our," or "us") is a digital products business that sells personal finance spreadsheet templates and publishes personal finance educational content on Pinterest. Our website is thecalmbudget.github.io and our store is at payhip.com/TheCalmBudget.

2. Information We Collect

  • Email address — when you opt in to receive our free budget starter template or newsletter via beehiiv.
  • Purchase information — processed by Payhip or Etsy. We never see or store your payment card details; those are handled by Payhip's and Etsy's payment processors.
  • Pinterest API access — our Pinterest scheduling tool uses the Pinterest API to post pre-approved content pins to our own Pinterest boards. It does not collect data about Pinterest users other than the account owner.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Email: to deliver the free template you requested, send our weekly newsletter, and occasionally mention products relevant to personal finance. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.
  • Pinterest API: solely to schedule and publish pins to boards we own. We do not sell, share, or store Pinterest data beyond what is needed to post content.

4. Pinterest API — Specific Disclosure

Our application uses the Pinterest API v5 for the following purposes only:

  • Reading our own boards to confirm the target board exists before posting.
  • Creating pins on our own boards with pre-approved titles, descriptions, images, and destination URLs.

We do not access follower data, private messages, analytics of other users, or any data beyond what is necessary to post content to our own account. Access tokens are stored locally on our own device in a private environment file and are never transmitted to any third-party server we control.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers as necessary to operate our business:

  • Payhip — to process purchases and deliver digital files.
  • Etsy — to process marketplace purchases.
  • beehiiv — to manage our email newsletter.
  • Pinterest — via the Pinterest API to post content on our own boards.

6. Data Retention

Email addresses are retained until you unsubscribe. One-click unsubscribe is included in every newsletter. You may also request deletion of any personal data we hold by contacting us directly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website (thecalmbudget.github.io) does not use cookies or tracking scripts. Our Payhip store and beehiiv newsletter may use cookies as governed by their own privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
